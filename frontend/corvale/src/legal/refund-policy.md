This page covers paid Corvale plans specifically. It works alongside the [Terms of
Service](./terms.md), which covers the rest of the agreement, and never promises less than our
payment processor guarantees every purchase.

## How cancellation works

Cancelling from Settings → Billing stops future charges but does not cut off access early: you
keep your plan through the end of the period you already paid for, and it simply does not renew.
Cancel any time before the renewal date to avoid the next charge.

Deleting your account is different from cancelling - see **Deleting your account** below.

## Refunds

**You can request a refund within 14 days of any charge**, including a renewal, no reason
required. This matches the minimum our payment processor guarantees on every purchase it
processes, so it is a floor we cannot go below even if we wanted to.

Outside that window, we consider refund requests individually - a mischarge, a duplicate
subscription, or something that was clearly our fault gets refunded; "I forgot to cancel" months
after the fact generally does not, though we would rather hear from you than have you dispute the
charge with your bank (see **Disputes** below, where that difference matters).

To ask for one, email support@corvale.app with the email address on your account. Once approved,
card refunds typically take a few business days to appear on your statement; the exact timing
depends on your bank, not on us.

## What a refund does to your access

**A refund does not automatically change your subscription or your access.** You keep your plan
as normal unless we tell you otherwise when we process the refund - we may downgrade an account by
hand in an individual case, but nothing happens to your access automatically just because a refund
was issued.

## Disputes

A dispute (also called a chargeback) is different from a refund: it means you contested a charge
directly with your card issuer or bank instead of asking us. **The moment we're notified of a
dispute, we end your paid access immediately**, before the dispute is even resolved - including if
it is later decided in our favor. We are not trying to be punitive; a disputed charge is being
argued with your bank, not with us, and that does not change no matter how it is eventually
decided, so we cannot wait for an outcome before acting.

If you dispute a charge by mistake, or a dispute is resolved in our favor and you would like the
subscription back, email support@corvale.app and we will sort it out by hand rather than making
you start over.

If you think a charge is wrong, asking us for a refund first is always faster and less disruptive
to your account than disputing it with your bank.

## Deleting your account

Deleting your account (Settings → Delete Account) cancels your subscription and stops all future
charges immediately, but it does **not** automatically refund the unused portion of your current
paid period. If you want that refunded, ask before you delete - once your account is gone, we no
longer have a subscription to look up in order to process one.
