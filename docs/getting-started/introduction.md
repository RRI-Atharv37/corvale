## What is spndr?

**spndr** is a personal finance web application that helps you track income, expenses, account balances, and savings. It is designed for students and young adults who want a straightforward way to understand where their money goes and how much they can safely spend.

## What you can do with spndr

With spndr, you can:

- Create a secure account and sign in from any browser
- View a dashboard summary of your financial picture at a glance
- Record income entries such as salary, freelance pay, or gifts
- Record expense entries with categories, payment methods, and tags
- Set up financial accounts (checking, cash, credit, savings) with opening balances
- Move money from your spendable balance into a saver pool
- Roll over saver funds at month-end with pushover and review your rollover history

## How spndr is organized

spndr groups your finances into clear areas:

| Area | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| **Dashboard** | High-level summary cards and quick links |
| **Income** | Money you receive |
| **Expense** | Money you spend |
| **Accounts** | Where your money lives (checking, cash, credit, savings) |
| **Saver** | A dedicated pool you allocate from spendable balance |
| **Pushover** | Month-end rollover of your saver balance into history |
