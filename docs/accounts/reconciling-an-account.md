## Match spndr against your bank statement

Reconciliation confirms that your transaction history in spndr agrees with your bank's statement, so nothing was missed or double-entered.

## Starting a reconciliation

1. Go to **Accounts** and click the reconcile icon on the account you want to check.
2. Enter the **statement end date** and **statement balance** from your bank statement.
3. Click **Compare to statement**.

spndr shows your cleared balance, your pending (uncleared) balance, the statement balance you entered, and the **differential** between your cleared balance and the statement - zero means they match.

## Clearing transactions

Below the comparison, every unreconciled transaction on the account is listed. Click a transaction to toggle it between **pending** and **cleared** as you check it off against your statement. A running "live cleared total" updates as you go, so you can watch the differential close in on zero.

## Finishing up

Once your cleared total matches the statement balance, click **Mark cleared transactions as reconciled**. This locks in every cleared transaction dated on or before the statement end date, marking them reconciled so they won't be included in your next comparison.

## Reconciliation history

Past reconciliation sessions for the account are listed below the comparison tool, showing the statement date, statement balance, and differential for each one.
