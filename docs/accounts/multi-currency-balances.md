## See every account in your preferred currency

If you hold accounts in more than one currency, spndr can show a converted balance alongside each account's native balance, using exchange rates you provide.

## Setting exchange rates

Open **Settings** (the gear icon in the header) and find the **exchange rates** section:

1. Click **Add rate**.
2. Choose the **from** and **to** currencies.
3. Enter the **rate** (how many units of the "to" currency equal one unit of the "from" currency).
4. Save.

You can edit or delete a rate at any time. spndr only needs the rate in one direction - if you set USD to EUR, it can also convert EUR balances back to USD automatically.

## Viewing converted balances

On the **Accounts** page, any account whose currency doesn't match your preferred currency shows a converted amount (`≈ 1,234.56 USD`) next to its native balance. If you haven't set a rate for that currency pair yet, spndr shows a warning instead of a converted amount so you know a conversion is missing.

Your preferred currency is set from your account preferences and also determines net worth and dashboard totals when you hold accounts in more than one currency.
