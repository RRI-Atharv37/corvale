## Dig deeper into your spending

The **Reports** page is where spndr's analytics live - charts and breakdowns that go beyond the stat cards on your dashboard Home. Navigate to Reports from the sidebar or go to `/reports`.

## What you'll find

- **Averages** - your typical income and spending over the selected period
- **Largest expenses** - your biggest individual transactions
- **Spending trends** - how spending moves over time
- **Income vs. expense** - side-by-side comparison per period
- **Savings rate** - the share of income you kept
- **Recurring totals** - what your recurring rules add up to
- **Budget analysis** and **spending analysis** - deeper cuts of your budget and category data
- **Crossover point** - where your savings trend and spending trend intersect

## Choosing a period

Use the period controls at the top of the page to switch between **Monthly**, **Yearly**, or a **Custom** date range. Every chart on the page updates to reflect your selection.
