## Stay on top of what needs attention

Corvale surfaces important events through the notification bell in the header. Click it to open a dropdown panel listing your recent notifications, newest first.

## What triggers a notification

| Notification | Fires when |
|---------------|------------|
| **Budget over limit** | A posted expense pushes a budget's spending past its limit |
| **Bill due** | An active recurring expense rule has a due date coming up within a few days |
| **Savings milestone** | A savings goal contribution crosses the 25%, 50%, 75%, or 100% mark |
| **Workspace invite** | Someone invites you to join their workspace |

Corvale avoids duplicate notifications for the same event - for example, a budget only notifies you once per period, and a savings goal only notifies once per milestone.

## Managing notifications

- Click a notification to jump to the relevant page (Budgets, Recurring, Savings Goals, or Workspaces).
- Click the check icon to mark a single notification as read.
- Click the **X** to dismiss a notification and remove it from the list.
- Click **Mark all read** to clear the unread badge in one action.

The bell icon shows a badge with your unread count, capped at "9+".
