## Share your finances with others

By default, everything you create in Corvale is **personal** - visible only to you. **Workspaces** let you share accounts, transactions, budgets, savings goals, and recurring rules with other people, like a household or roommates splitting bills.

Navigate to Workspaces from the sidebar or go to `/workspaces`.

## Personal vs. workspace scope

Use the **workspace switcher** in the sidebar to move between **Personal** and any workspace you belong to. Whichever scope is active determines what accounts, transactions, budgets, and other resources you see and create - a workspace never mixes with your personal data, and your personal data isn't visible to other workspace members.

## What you can do

On the Workspaces page, you can:

- Create a new workspace
- Invite people to a workspace you own by email
- View and manage members and their roles
- Accept or decline invitations you've received
- Leave a workspace, or remove a member if you're the owner
