title: Creating and Inviting
---

## Start a shared workspace

1. Navigate to **Workspaces** from the sidebar.
2. Click **Create workspace**.
3. Enter a **name** for the workspace.
4. Click **Create** to save.

You're automatically added as the workspace's **owner**.

## Inviting members

From your workspace's page in the **Workspaces** tab:

1. Enter the **email** of the person you want to invite.
2. Choose a **role** - Editor or Viewer.
3. Send the invite.

You can't invite someone who is already a member, invite yourself, or send a second invite to someone with a pending one.

## Accepting or declining an invite

Switch to the **Invitations** tab (`/workspaces?tab=invitations`) to see invites sent to you. Each one can be **accepted** - which adds the workspace to your switcher - or **declined**.

You'll also get a [notification](../notifications/overview.md) when someone invites you.

## Leaving or removing a member

- Any member can leave a workspace they belong to, except the owner - an owner must transfer or delete the workspace first.
- The owner can remove any other member at any time.
