## Who can do what

Every workspace member has one of three roles:

| Role | Can view | Can create/edit/delete | Can manage members |
|------|----------|--------------------------|----------------------|
| **Owner** | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| **Editor** | Yes | Yes | No |
| **Viewer** | Yes | No | No |

Only the owner can invite members, change roles, or remove members. There is exactly one owner per workspace, and the owner's own role can't be changed by anyone.

## What viewers see

If you have viewer access to a workspace, Corvale shows a banner explaining that your access is read-only. Create, edit, and delete controls are hidden throughout the app while you're in that workspace's scope - you can still switch back to Personal or another workspace where you have edit access.
