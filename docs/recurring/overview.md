## Automate your regular income and bills

The **Recurring** page lets you set up rules for income and expenses that repeat on a schedule - a paycheck, a subscription, rent. Instead of re-entering the same transaction every period, Corvale generates draft transactions for you to review and post.

Navigate to Recurring from the sidebar or go to `/recurring`.

## What you can do

On the Recurring page, you can:

- Create rules for recurring income or expenses with a schedule
- Generate draft transactions for rules that are due
- Review, confirm, or dismiss drafts in your draft inbox
- Edit or cancel a rule at any time
- Archive rules you no longer need
- See upcoming due dates on the recurring calendar

## How recurring rules work

A recurring rule does not post transactions automatically. It defines a schedule and a template - amount, account, category, tags - and Corvale calculates the rule's next due date. When you generate drafts, Corvale creates a draft `Transaction` for each due date up to today and advances the rule's next due date past them. Drafts sit in your inbox until you confirm or dismiss them, so nothing affects your account balance until you approve it.

See [Creating a Recurring Rule](./creating-a-recurring-rule.md) and [Managing Drafts](./managing-drafts.md) for the details.
