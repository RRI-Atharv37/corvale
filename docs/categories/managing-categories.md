## Edit, reorder, default, and archive your categories

Once you have sub-categories, you can maintain them from the Categories page.

## Editing a category

1. Find the category in its master group.
2. Click the **edit** (pencil) icon.
3. Update the name, icon, or color.
4. Click **Save**.

You cannot change which master category a sub-category belongs to after creation. Archive the old category and create a new one under the correct master if you need to move it.

## Setting a default category

1. Click the **star** icon on a category row, or enable **Set as default** in the edit modal.
2. spndr clears the previous default and marks the selected category as default.

The default category may pre-select in the transaction Category picker when you create new entries.

## Reordering categories

Use the **move up** and **move down** controls within a master group to change sort order. Order affects how categories appear in the Categories list and in the transaction picker.

## Archiving a category

1. Click the **archive** (trash) icon on a category row.
2. Confirm in the dialog.

Archived categories:

- Disappear from the Categories list and transaction picker
- Remain linked to existing transactions
- Lose the default flag if they were default

Archiving is a soft delete. spndr does not permanently remove category records from the database.

## Master categories are read-only

The nine master categories (Food, Transport, and so on) are system-defined. You cannot rename, recolor, or archive them. Create sub-categories under masters to customize your setup.
