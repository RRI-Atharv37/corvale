## Return money to spendable balance

If you need access to funds you previously set aside, you can withdraw from the saver back to your spendable balance.

## Step-by-step withdrawal

1. Navigate to the **Saver** page.
2. In the **Withdraw from saver** card on the right, enter the amount you want to withdraw.
3. Click **Withdraw**.

On success, Corvale decreases your saver balance, raises your **displayed** spendable balance by the same amount, and shows a success notification. As with a deposit, no money moves and no transaction is created - only the earmark changes.

## Rules and limits

- You cannot withdraw more than your current saver balance.
- The withdrawal amount must be greater than zero.
- If your saver balance is $0.00, the **Withdraw** button is disabled.

## What withdrawal does not affect

Withdrawing from the saver:

- Does **not** change your net worth
- Does **not** create an income or expense entry
- Does **not** modify any account balances

It simply removes the earmark, so the amount is once again counted as spendable in Corvale's display. Your bank account is untouched.

## When to withdraw

Common reasons to withdraw from the saver:

- You need the funds for an unexpected expense
- You over-allocated to the saver and want to rebalance
- You are correcting a deposit mistake
