title: Creating a Savings Goal
---

## Define a target and optional schedule

You create savings goals from the **Savings Goals** page. Each goal stores a name, target amount, and optional deadline. You can also link an account for reference and configure automatic contributions.

## Step-by-step

1. Navigate to **Savings Goals** from the sidebar.
2. Click **Create goal**.
3. Complete the form fields below.
4. Click **Create goal** to save.

On success, Corvale closes the modal, shows a success notification, and displays the new goal on the **Active** tab with $0.00 saved toward the target.

## Form fields

### Goal name (required)

A descriptive name such as "Emergency fund" or "New laptop."

### Target amount and currency (required)

Enter a positive target (for example, `2,000.00`) and choose the currency. The default currency comes from your **Settings** preference.

### Target date (optional)

Set a deadline if you want Corvale to calculate **required monthly contribution** and **months remaining** metrics. Leave empty for open-ended goals.

### Linked account (optional)

Associate the goal with one of your active accounts for context on the goal card. Linking an account does not automatically move money or sync balances - it is informational only.

### Automatic contributions (optional)

Enable **Enable automatic contributions** to schedule recurring deposits toward the goal:

| Field | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Auto contribution amount** | Positive amount added each interval |
| **Interval** | **Weekly** or **Monthly** |
| **Day of month** | For monthly intervals, the day (1–28) when a contribution is due |

When an auto contribution is due, the goal card shows an **Auto due** badge. Click the refresh icon on the card to process the scheduled contribution.

Automatic contributions are blocked while a goal is paused, completed, or archived.

## After creation

New goals start with:

- **Current amount:** $0.00
- **Status:** Active
- **Progress:** 0% complete

Add manual contributions or process auto contributions to increase progress. When contributions reach the target, Corvale can automatically mark the goal complete.

## Editing goals

Click the **pencil** icon on a goal card to update the name, target, deadline, linked account, or auto-contribution settings. Increasing the target above the current saved amount may reopen a completed goal.
