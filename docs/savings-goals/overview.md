## Track progress toward named targets

The **Savings Goals** page helps you define financial targets - an emergency fund, a laptop, tuition, or any goal with a dollar amount - and log contributions over time. Unlike the **Saver** pool (a discretionary monthly sweep), savings goals are named targets with optional deadlines, progress metrics, and a contribution history.

Navigate to Savings Goals from the sidebar or go to `/savings-goals`.

## What you can do

On the Savings Goals page, you can:

- Create goals with a target amount, optional deadline, and optional linked account
- Log manual contributions with optional notes
- Enable scheduled automatic contributions (weekly or monthly)
- View progress bars, required monthly contribution, and projected completion date
- Pause, resume, complete, or archive goals
- Review contribution history in a timeline modal
- Filter goals by **Active**, **Completed**, or **Archived** tabs

## Savings goals vs saver vs pushover

| Feature | Purpose |
|---------|---------|
| **Savings Goals** | Named targets with progress tracking and contribution history |
| **Saver** | Percentage or custom deposits from spendable balance during the month |
| **Pushover** | Month-end snapshot that archives saver balance and resets the pool |

spndr keeps Saver and Pushover as the monthly discretionary sweep. Savings goals are a separate feature for goal-oriented saving. Depositing to Saver does not automatically update a savings goal unless you record a contribution.

## Goal statuses

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Active** | Goal is open; you can contribute and edit settings |
| **Paused** | Contributions and auto-contributions are blocked until you resume |
| **Completed** | Target amount reached or manually marked complete |
| **Archived** | Removed from active lists; kept for reference |
