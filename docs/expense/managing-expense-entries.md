## Edit, delete, and browse expenses

Manage your spending records directly from the Expense list page.

## Viewing entries

Each expense entry in the list shows:

- **Title** - the entry name
- **Date** - formatted as "MMM D, YYYY"
- **Category** - always shown (required field)
- **Payment method** - if provided, shown after the category
- **Amount** - displayed in rose/red on the right side

## Editing an entry

1. Find the entry in the list.
2. Click the **pencil icon** on the right side of the row.
3. The edit modal opens with all current values pre-filled, including tags joined as a comma-separated string.
4. Make your changes and click **Update**.

## Deleting an entry

1. Find the entry in the list.
2. Click the **trash icon** on the right side of the row.
3. Confirm deletion in the dialog that appears.

Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

## Pagination behavior

- Entries display **10 per page**.
- Deleting the last entry on a page (when not on page 1) moves you to the previous page.
- Adding a new entry resets to page 1.

## Empty state

If you have no expense entries, Corvale shows:

- **Title:** No expenses yet
- **Description:** Add your first expense to start tracking spending.
