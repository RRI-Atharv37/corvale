## Set spending limits and track progress

The **Budgets** page helps you define spending limits for a calendar month or custom date range, then compare those limits against your actual posted expenses. You can scope a budget to all spending or to a single category, and optionally limit tracking to specific accounts.

Navigate to Budgets from the sidebar or go to `/budgets`.

## What you can do

On the Budgets page, you can:

- Create monthly or custom-period budgets with an overall or category scope
- Limit a budget to all accounts or a selected subset
- View progress bars showing spent amount, remaining amount, and percent used
- See over-budget warnings when spending exceeds the limit
- Edit active budgets or archive budgets you no longer need
- Switch between **Active** and **History** tabs to review current and past budgets

## How budgets relate to transactions

Budget progress counts **posted expense** transactions only:

- **Income** and **transfer** transactions do not affect budget spent totals
- **Draft** transactions are excluded until they are posted (draft generation ships in a future recurring-bills feature)
- **Split expenses** attribute child line amounts toward category budgets; overall budgets count the parent total without double-counting split children

If you have not recorded expenses in the budget period yet, progress shows $0.00 spent.

## Budget vs saver vs savings goals

| Feature | Purpose |
|---------|---------|
| **Budgets** | Limit and monitor spending against a category or overall total |
| **Saver** | Move discretionary funds from spendable balance into a monthly savings pool |
| **Savings Goals** | Track named targets with deadlines and contribution history |

Budgets do not move money. They measure spending against a limit you set.
