## Get set up on your first visit

The first time you sign in, spndr walks you through a short onboarding wizard to get your account ready to use. The wizard can appear on any page after you sign in.

## The steps

1. **Account** - create your first account (name, type, and opening balance).
2. **Categories** - a quick look at your pre-seeded categories, so you know where to find them.
3. **Budget** *(optional)* - create a first budget, or skip this step.
4. **Goal** *(optional)* - create a first savings goal, or skip this step.
5. **Tour** - a closing summary pointing you toward Transactions, Reports, and other key pages.

A progress bar at the top of the wizard tracks how far along you are. You can skip the whole wizard at any step using **Skip onboarding** - your account works the same either way.

## Replaying the tour

Want to see it again? Open **Settings** (the gear icon in the header) and click **Replay onboarding tour** under the Account section. This restarts the wizard from the first step, regardless of whether you completed or skipped it before.
